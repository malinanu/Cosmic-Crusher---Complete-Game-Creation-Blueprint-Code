    HOW TO PLAY
    • Spin the planet so aliens roll under your astronaut. Fast enough = SPLAT!
    • Too slow and they hurt you. Watch the ROLL SPEED gauge: the white notch is crushing speed. Stay out of the red too long and you stumble.
    • BOOST/SLAM jumps; landing sends a shockwave that crushes anything nearby. Press again in the air to slam down fast. Dodge the boss's goo by jumping!
    • Crushes fill the SUPER BAR. Then hit SHIELD/CRUSH: shield up and everything you touch is crushed.
    • Object Selector swaps planets: Mars is heavy, Earth balanced, Moon fast and slippery.
