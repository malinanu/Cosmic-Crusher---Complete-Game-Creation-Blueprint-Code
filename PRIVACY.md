# Cosmic Crusher privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Cosmic Crusher does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No accounts and no names.** The game never asks for a name, email, age or any other personal detail. The "top runs" list shows only scores and level numbers.
- **No network use.** The game makes no network requests. It contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services. Everything it needs ships inside the app.
- **Where your progress is saved:**

  | Version | Saved to |
  |---|---|
  | Android or iPhone app | the app's own storage on your device |
  | YouTube Playables | your YouTube account's cloud save, handled by YouTube under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
  | web browser | your browser's local storage |

  Saved progress includes unlocked levels, best scores, top runs and your settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes it.
- **Permissions.** On Android the app declares the standard internet permission that its built-in web view requires; the game itself does not go online. It requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts or file access. The motion sensor is read only if you choose tilt controls, and readings never leave the device. Vibration is used for feedback, and it can be turned off in Settings.
- **Children.** The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children.

Questions about this policy: open an issue in this repository.
